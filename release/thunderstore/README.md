# LWF Raven QoL

A QoL mod for Delivery Ravens in **Lazy Witch's Factory**.

![Drag placement](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KiyonakaNata/lwf-raven-qol/main/img/chain-drag.webp)

## Drag placement

Place a Raven, drag, and release. Pairs of "Dispatch Port + Raven" are placed at maximum carrying range. Release on an existing Dispatch Port to share it, or on a building with an input port to connect to it.

## Retarget a Raven

Point at a placed Raven and press the `Toggle Dispatch Port Setup` key (default `Tab`), then click a new spot, share an existing port, or drag a new line. The old Dispatch Port stays, and leftover items can be taken out with conveyors.

![Retargeting](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KiyonakaNata/lwf-raven-qol/main/img/retarget.webp)

## See what's inside

Point at a Raven or a Dispatch Port to see what it holds, as icon × count.

![Hover stock panel](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KiyonakaNata/lwf-raven-qol/main/img/stock-hover.png)

## Map view

Three buttons at the top left: redeploy Momoko, a stock overlay for every Raven and Dispatch Port on screen, and a toggle for the left-side panels. Control hints and the sponsor list are hidden while in map view.

![Map view buttons](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KiyonakaNata/lwf-raven-qol/main/img/mapview.webp)

---

Settings live in `BepInEx/config/kiyonakanata.lwfravenqol.cfg`. Full documentation: [GitHub](https://github.com/KiyonakaNata/lwf-raven-qol).

Built and tested on Lazy Witch's Factory **ver 0.24.1**. Unofficial mod, not supported by the developer.
