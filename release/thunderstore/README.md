# LWF Raven QoL

A QoL mod for Delivery Ravens in **Lazy Witch's Factory**.

## Drag placement

Place a Raven, drag, release. Pairs of "Dispatch Port + Raven" line up at maximum carrying range. Release on a Dispatch Port to share it, or on an input port to connect.

![Drag placement](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KiyonakaNata/lwf-raven-qol/main/img/chain-drag.webp)

## Retarget a Raven

Point at a Raven, press `Toggle Dispatch Port Setup` (default `Tab`), then click, share, or drag a new line. The old Dispatch Port stays.

![Retargeting](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KiyonakaNata/lwf-raven-qol/main/img/retarget.webp)

## See what's inside

Point at a Raven or Dispatch Port — its items show as icon × count.

![Hover stock panel](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KiyonakaNata/lwf-raven-qol/main/img/stock-hover.png)

## Map view

Three buttons at the top left: redeploy Momoko, a stock overlay, and a left-panel toggle. Control hints and sponsors are hidden.

![Map view buttons](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KiyonakaNata/lwf-raven-qol/main/img/mapview.webp)
